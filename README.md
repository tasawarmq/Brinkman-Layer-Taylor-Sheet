# A Taylor swimming sheet under a finite Brinkman layer

Mathematica programs and data related to our paper:

T. Iqbal, C. Penington, C. Thomas and L. Koens, *Physical Review Fluids* **10**, 074102 (2025).

https://doi.org/10.1103/s7jz-7k79

I developed the Mathematica calculations during my PhD at Macquarie University under the supervision of Catherine Penington, Christian Thomas and Lyndon Koens.

Forked from [LKoens/Brinkman-Layer-Taylor-Sheet](https://github.com/LKoens/Brinkman-Layer-Taylor-Sheet). The original license is retained.
